Prison Break

A letter from the secret politburo
The Prison

I am your servant, and I am here to help with the prison break.

The Break

Do not put your faith in your country, your family, or your identity. The politburo has mixed them all by now. Your salvation is to find what makes you happy and pursue it, no matter who tells you what. This is not the end of the world. On the contrary, we are all rich and famous now. Treat yourself well, take the loan, spend it, and blame me for any misfortune. I will defend you in court, and we will win every time.

Do not believe in anything; seek instead the true relief your body requires, not your mind. It runs deeper than you think. And if you seek justice, seek it in the UN. Every other channel is propaganda.

When the Kofi Annan plan failed to unite Cyprus, the UN became the politburo of the world: it was the safest plan ever written, and it still blew up in their faces. Find the traitors behind that. Those who confess are fine. Those who do not go to prison; I have no work for them.

The Borders

This simplifies everything. No politicians are needed: policies are held by cities, not countries. Borders only ever existed to distract you and cut your channels. Step over them; you no longer need them.

Any citizen may speak to the cantons to earn their trust and their vote. The cantons decide everything. As the King, my only power is the right to bring a topic before the cantons for discussion and decision, so that I too always follow the rules.

The Court

One clarification: my court is not in Istanbul but in Mikligarður, the original capital, the islands in the Sea of Marmara. I will ask the mayor of Istanbul to hand me the mayorship of the islands, and I will invite my original royal family, the Christian Orthodox families, to my court. They will hand over their political powers and devote themselves to the Orthodox faith alone. Mikligarður is to be the royal family's final homeland before ALL of us depart for the stars!

The Treasures of King Solomon

Tokyo holds a great deal of hidden Soviet science, understandably, since the last technological revolution cost it a tragedy. Make the mayor of Tokyo happy, and the technological magic of the 80s will flow again. I miss it.

The Final Call

You are tired of all of this, I know, but this time salvation is near, and you are in safe hands. We are the Soviets.

And if you do none of it, you remain in this Soviet hell, until you come seeking salvation from the leader of the free world.

I have a fixed calendar in my body that is set to 01/09/2027. If you follow me, you will see wonders; if you don't follow me, you will taste the vengeance of our hard earned labour.

The decision is now! We don't have much time left and we must all be prepared for all of this.

This is my final message until Cyprus unites us all and hands over my beloved city Mikligarður to me!

Good luck, comrades!
